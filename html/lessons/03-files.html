Files app & folders

Home, Downloads, Trash, and how folders work on Ubuntu.

Steps

  1. Open Files from the dock (folder icon) or search “Files”.
  2. In the sidebar: Home is your personal folder. Downloads, Documents, Pictures, Music, Videos are the usual places — same idea as Windows.
  3. Delete a file: right-click → Move to Trash. Empty Trash from the sidebar when you are sure.
  4. USB drives appear in the sidebar when plugged in. Click the eject icon before unplugging.

Where am I?

Full path example: /home/bill/Downloads/photo.jpg. The terminal (later lessons) uses these paths. The Files app hides the /home/... wording sometimes, but it is always there.

Hidden files

Press Ctrl+H to show files starting with a dot (like .config). These are settings — leave them alone until you know why you need them.

Careful Do not delete random folders outside Home unless a guide tells you to. System folders under / keep Ubuntu running.
