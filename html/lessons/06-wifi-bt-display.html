Wi‑Fi, Bluetooth, displays

Connect to networks, pair devices, and set resolution / scaling.

Steps

  1. Click the system menu (top-right) → Wi‑Fi → pick your network → enter the password.
  2. Bluetooth: system menu or Settings → Bluetooth → turn on → pair headphones or a mouse when they are in pairing mode.
  3. Displays: Settings → Displays. Set resolution, orientation, and (on HiDPI screens) scale 100% / 200% until text looks comfortable.
  4. Multiple monitors: arrange them in the Displays diagram to match your desk, then Apply.

No Wi‑Fi listed?

Check airplane mode, whether you are on Ethernet instead, or if proprietary drivers are needed (Settings → Additional Drivers). Lesson 12 has a short checklist.

Laptop tip Function keys often control brightness and volume — look for icons on the keys. Fn lock behavior varies by laptop brand.
